# Impeccable Design Context — Siam Pinnacle Group AI Learning Suite

## Design Context

### Users
1. **Frontline Employees (85% of Wave 1 — 12,000 retail store associates & packaging plant operators in Thailand):**
   - **Context:** Using a personal smartphone on cellular/store Wi-Fi during a 10-minute shift break, arriving via a one-tap deep link from **LINE Official Account**. Bright fluorescent store lighting or factory floor lighting.
   - **Job to be done:** Complete a 5-minute skill check without anxiety, see what skills are needed for their next career step (e.g., *Senior Store Associate → Store Supervisor*), follow a practical 70-20-10 plan, and ask a polite Thai/English AI coach questions right beside a lesson.
   - **Emotional goal:** **Encouraged, safe, and clear**—never judged, surveilled, or overwhelmed by corporate jargon.

2. **Frontline Store & Plant Managers:**
   - **Context:** Time-poor supervisors managing 8–25 direct reports on the floor; reviewing approvals between shifts on mobile.
   - **Job to be done:** Calibrate self-assessments against quiz checks, batch-approve on-track 12-week plans, and sign off on-the-job (`70%`) project evidence within a 5-day SLA.
   - **Emotional goal:** **In control, fast, and fair**—able to spot discrepancies and SLA risks in 3 seconds.

3. **HR, L&D & Business Unit Leaders:**
   - **Context:** Desktop/tablet review preparing for the Week 12 Group CEO Leadership Forum.
   - **Job to be done:** Inspect adoption funnel across 12,000 employees, capability heatmaps (Baseline vs. Week 8), operational KPI correlations (shrinkage, OEE), and AI coach safety/cost health while respecting PDPA anonymity ($n \ge 10$).
   - **Emotional goal:** **Analytical confidence and boardroom readiness**.

### Brand Personality
- **Three Concrete Words:** **Tactile · Grounded · Shift-Ready**
- **Physical Metaphor:** A well-crafted, high-contrast **store operations field handbook** paired with the familiarity of a **LINE Mini-App**—warm paper surfaces, deep Siamjade/teal ink, amber calibration markers, and crisp tabular numerals.
- **Voice & Tone:** Warm, respectful everyday Thai (`ค่ะ / นะคะ`) and plain, direct English. Zero corporate fluff.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme:** High-craft **Warm Daylight Mode** (engineered for bright retail store and packaging plant environments where dark mode causes halation and glare on budget Android screens), using **OKLCH perceptually uniform tokens** with teal-tinted warm paper neutrals (`hue ~192°`, never pure `#ffffff` or dead `#000000`).
- **Typography:**
  - **Display / Headings & Numerals:** `Bricolage Grotesque` (distinctive, high-character grotesque with crisp geometric numerals, replacing generic `Inter`).
  - **UI Body (Latin):** `Plus Jakarta` / `Inter` are banned by Impeccable; we use `Figtree` (open, high x-height humanist sans built for small mobile screens).
  - **UI Body & Display (Thai):** `Anuphan` (Cadson Demak's modern loopless Thai UI typeface designed for digital clarity at 12–16px) with `Noto Sans Thai` fallback and `1.65` line-height to protect Thai tone marks (`วรรณยุกต์`).
- **References (from Mobbin & EdTech Research):**
  - **Duolingo & Fluently:** Low-stakes, one-screen-at-a-time diagnostic skill check with segmented progress bar, tactile radio cards, and a "calibration mirror" review step before submission.
  - **Open edX Pathways & LearnPath AI:** Living 12-week roadmap with a connected vertical timeline spine, 70-20-10 mix breakdown bar, and transparent "Why AI picked this" rationale.
  - **Perplexity Mobile & Mobbin iOS Bottom Sheet:** Interactive inline citation pills (`[1]`) that open a non-blocking **Source Inspection Bottom Sheet** for both lessons and programme policy documents, plus a structured **Two-Layer Guardrail Status Strip**.
  - **ReUI Approval Inbox & Nockerl Approval Anatomy:** SLA-tiered manager queue with avatar initials, diff-highlighted calibration table (`Self` vs `Quiz` vs `Final`), and 44px-compliant stepper controls.
- **Anti-References:**
  - Generic AI dark-mode neon gradients, glassmorphic slop, gradient text (`background-clip: text`), and `border-left: 4px solid` side stripes (strictly banned).
  - Bloated desktop LMS tables crammed onto a 390px phone screen.

### Design Principles
1. **One Primary Action Per Mobile Fold:** Frontline staff on a 10-minute break should immediately see their readiness ring, their single next priority step, and one-tap entry into the lesson or coach.
2. **Make AI Constraints Visible:** Every AI output shows *why* it is trustworthy—70-20-10 catalogue badges on paths, clickable source drawers (`[S1]`) on coach answers, and explicit guardrail shields when out-of-scope queries are intercepted.
3. **Calibration Over Guesswork:** Wherever levels appear (`L1`–`L4`), pair numbers with human meaning, visual progress bars, and clear diffs between self-rating, quiz check, and role requirement.
4. **4pt Spatial Rhythm & 44px Touch Ergonomics:** Use a strict 4pt spacing scale, `:focus-visible` keyboard rings, `tabular-nums` on every metric, and generous touch targets for one-handed thumb use.
