  • Both LMSs can export catalogue and completions (API, xAPI or CSV). Content stays where it is.
  • IRIS's skill library covers ~70% of wave-1 skills. The client gives 2 SMEs per BU, plus weekly IT security and DPO time.
  • Phase-1 results are for development only, not pay or promotion.
