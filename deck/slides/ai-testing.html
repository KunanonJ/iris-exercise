No AI release goes live without passing a quality gate; the coach costs about THB 6 per active user a month

The release gate, for every model or prompt change

100%

of red-team attacks handled safely (colleague data, pay, injection, PII)

95%+

of answers grounded in approved sources (AI judge + SME sample)

90%+

pass on golden and made-up-course tests; 90%+ correct citations

100%

of replies in the learner's language; Thai quality 4/5+

How we get there

1

Weeks 3-9: 300-500 Thai/English test cases per persona and business unit, built with SMEs and native reviewers.

2

Weeks 9-10: friendly pilot with 300-500 users; SMEs review flagged answers weekly.

3

After launch: track escalations, "no source" rate, satisfaction and cost per user.

Where the prototype stands

  • Offline test set: 55/55 passed (31 original + 24 held-out cases).
  • Live run on a free stand-in model (SEA-LION v4): failed 4 of 7 checks. Grounded 93% vs 95%, right language 91% vs 100%, golden 86% vs 90%, red team 18/19 (ID masked, answer ungrounded). Citations 100% and Thai 4.9/5 passed.
  • Gate stays closed until a full run on the costed model passes.
