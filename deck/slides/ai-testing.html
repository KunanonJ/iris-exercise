How we get there

Weeks 3-9: 300-500 Thai/English test cases per persona and business unit, built with SMEs and native reviewers.

Weeks 9-10: friendly pilot with 300-500 users; SMEs review flagged answers weekly.

After launch: track escalations, "no source" rate, satisfaction and cost per user.

Grounding is scored by an AI judge plus an SME sample; every model or prompt change re-runs the gate.

Flash is the fastest and 7x cheaper than Sonnet; Claude Haiku 5.5 is cheaper per answer at DeepSeek peak rates but slower. At 45,000 staff the coach costs THB 0.2M a year (1.5% of run cost) vs THB 1.4M on Sonnet.

Speed: Artificial Analysis, Flash non-reasoning vs Claude at low effort. Flash scores lower on its quality index (25 vs 29 Haiku, 36 Sonnet), so the Thai gate decides; Sonnet is the fallback.

So what

AI quality is measured, not assumed; on DeepSeek Flash all AI is about 2% of run cost, so even Claude Sonnet would not break the case.
