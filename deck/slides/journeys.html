The manager's journey

  1. Alerted when a team member's result, plan or project evidence is waiting.
  2. Reviews on the phone each of the three approvals (amber): approve, or return with a reason.
  3. No bottleneck: batch review for routine items; after 5 working days, escalation to the HRBP.
  4. Tracks the team on the manager view: skills, plans and progress.

Privacy by role: managers see direct reports only; HR and leaders see groups of 10 or more.

So what

Frontline staff need one tap and five minutes; managers keep control without becoming a bottleneck.
