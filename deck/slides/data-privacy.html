8

Privacy is on the critical path, so it starts in week 1: the DPIA is done by week 3 and the security pack goes to IT in week 2, not week 10. The coach is built so that the riskiest data never reaches the model at all: mask identity numbers, exclude salary and other employees' records; only own development scores may be supplied. Production safeguards still need validation.
